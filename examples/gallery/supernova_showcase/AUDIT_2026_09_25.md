# Audit of the Cas A reconstruction, 2026-09-25

This audit covers the Orlando-state track: `casa_xfit` fits N, O2, P and Q2, as described in `PLUTO150.md` §6b.

Method:
* Eight independent auditors worked in parallel on the fit machinery, the forward physics, the observation model, the reverse-shock estimator, the CR code, adjoint readiness, the CR literature and the observational targets.
* Every quantitative claim below was measured on the saved states and products (CPU/numpy), or quoted from a paper the auditors read.
* Full reports, scripts and paper extracts are in
`/export/data/lstorcks/casa_orlando150/work/audit_2026_09_25/`: `fit_audit.md`, `forward_physics.md`, `obs_model.md`, `cr_code.md`, `adjoint_readiness.md`, `lit_cr.md` and `lit_obs.md`.

## 1. Verdicts

1. **The "reverse shock at 31–42″ vs 96″" residual is an estimator artefact.** `casa_pluto_diff.edge_radii` takes the inner edge as an r⁻¹² power mean through a *sigmoid* window at 0.25 r_FS. Below the cut, the sigmoid tail times r⁻¹² grows toward the centre. As a result, 93–100 % of the integral comes from floor-temperature and warm interior gas at r < 0.25 r_FS.
   * Q2's 540 epoch×cone values all lie at 0.14–0.26 r_FS, at or below the cut.
   * The "resolution trend" (34″ at 128³ → 44″ at 256³) is only the sigmoid width shrinking.
   * On the evolved states, three physical estimators agree: the tracer (outer edge of the unshocked ejecta), the inner edge of the contiguous T > 10⁷ K layer, and a 50 %-hot crossing. Values of r_RS/r_FS by estimator:

     | state | tracer | contiguous hot layer | `edge_radii` |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | as delivered, 319 yr | 0.67 | 0.68–0.70 | 0.33 |
     | fit C, 380 yr | 0.65 | 0.66 | 0.30 |
     | fit H, 364 yr | 0.62 | 0.65–0.66 | 0.34 |

   * Cas A itself is at 0.62–0.72: Gotthelf+01 95/153″, Arias+18 114/163″, Helder & Vink 08 115/160″, Vink+22 1.9/2.8 pc.
   * r_RS was never a likelihood term, so the fits themselves are unbiased; only the interpretation was wrong.
   * Q2's own 2000 state still has to be re-measured with the corrected estimator.
2. **Cosmic-ray back-reaction is not the key missing factor.** Four constraints show this independently:
   * The γ-ray budget: Fermi, MAGIC, VERITAS and Yuan+13, rescaled to the post-shock n ≈ 3.6 cm⁻³, give W_p ≈ 0.5–2.8e50 erg, i.e. 2–14 % of E_SN. That caps γ_eff at ≈ 1.54–1.65 (a volume average) and the CR efficiency at ζ ≈ 5–15 %.
   * Self-similarity: a constant γ_eff or a constant escape fraction leaves m = (n−3)/(n−s), and 2/(5−s), unchanged. Only energy loss lowers m, and reaching m ≈ 0.6 would need ~10⁵⁰⁺ erg escaping. LHAASO bounds the escaped energy to < 4e47 (10/n) erg at 100–1000 TeV.
   * Published CR-hydro models of Cas A. Patnaude & Fesen 09 needed ≳ 30 % of E_SN in CRs for m = 0.66. Orlando+16 reach m ≈ 0.61 only at an η they themselves reject on the Fermi budget. Orlando+22 find that the FS/RS circles are "very similar" across η = 0, 1e-4 and 1e-3.
   * In-repo: Result 3 found r_RS/r_FS rises by at most +0.08, and only at the excluded γ = 4/3. Result 28 found no effect on the hard continuum.

   At the γ-ray bound the expected changes are:

   | quantity | change |
   |---|---|
   | m | −0.01 to −0.05 |
   | r_RS | +1 to +5 % |
   | FS–CD gap | −6 to −14 % |
   | T_post | −4 to −10 % (up to −50 % only through the Haggerty & Caprioli 2020 postcursor) |

   CRs are second-order missing physics. A full 3D FD two-fluid implementation is designed in `cr_code.md` §3–4 but not justified now. The existing FV 1D CR code is being repaired and tested, and a 1D in-house quantification has been run; see §5.

   The delivered PLUTO state itself has γ = 5/3: the forward-shock compression is 4.0–4.2 on 400 rays. It is Orlando's `W15-IIb-sh-MHD+dec-rl-hr` run, with MHD and radiative losses, not the γ_eff = 3/2 "favoured" model of Orlando+22.
3. **The fitted age (explosion ≈ 1635) is the initial condition's own clock, not Cas A's.**
   * The fit ends up at t ≈ m_model / μ_data, where the model's m is set by the W15 ejecta envelope (n ≈ 7, m_ED ≈ 0.8 after the shell passage).
   * The fit lowers m to ≈ 0.70 with a ×3.4 wind (n_H ≈ 2.5 cm⁻³ at 2.5 pc, 2–4× Lee+14), a wind slope of 1.67, E ×1.51 and M_ej ×1.34 (the Y_lm modes are not mass-neutral). It then needs 365 yr.
   * Orlando+22 themselves compared this model with Cas A at 350–370 yr.
   * The explosion date is bounded from below by the undecelerated knot convergence date: 1671.3 ± 0.9 (Thorstensen+01; deceleration only makes the true date later). Q2 violates this bound, as does its own ballistic convergence date t_expl + 145.5 (1 − 1/s_v) = 1655.
   * Q2's D = 3.13 kpc is 1.7–2.7σ below Alarie+14 (3.33 ± 0.10) and Reed+95 (3.4 +0.3 −0.1).
4. **The robust dynamical deficiency is energy, not cosmic rays.** At 1681 and 3.4 kpc, the remnant must have R_FS ≈ 2.62 pc. Every adiabatic ejecta-into-r⁻² solution with that radius has V_FS ≳ 6000 km/s (m ≈ 0.75–0.78, E ≈ 2.4–3.9e51). That sits within ~1σ of Vink+22's 5800 km/s and m = 0.73 ± 0.10, and it is the CALIBRATION Result 27 solution (E 2.43e51, M_ej 3.0). W15 has 1.5e51.

   The in-house expansion rate (0.294–0.312″/yr) is ~10 % below Vink+22 (0.218 %/yr ≈ 0.349″/yr). The in-house number is measured on 0.5–7 keV images about the wrong centre, so the in-house number, not Vink's, is suspect.

## 5. Stage 1 outcomes (2026-09-25)

Reports: `/export/data/lstorcks/casa_orlando150/work/stage1/*_REPORT_b.md` with `*_REVIEW_b.md`, plus `cr/REPORT.md`. Every code change was adversarially reviewed, and the reviewers fixed four further bugs.

* **CR, in-house 1D test** (`casa_cr_1d.py`; repaired two-fluid FV code; DSA at the forward shock of the Result-27 fiducial):
  * γ-ray-allowed ζ = 0.05–0.26: Δm = −0.0006 … −0.004 and Δ(r_RS/r_FS) = +0.002 … +0.011 at fixed E. At fixed r_FS, Δm = −0.002.
  * With escape (ζ = 0.3, f_esc = 0.5, fixed r_FS): Δm = −0.018 and Δ(r_RS/r_FS) = +0.029. That case lets 3e50 erg escape, above the escape bound.
  * Δm = −0.05 requires a pure energy sink of 1.3e51 erg (35 % of E).
  * **CR back-reaction is excluded as the key factor.**
  * Repairs:
    * `shock_finder` Mach estimate: two audit bugs, plus a third found here (downstream γ_eff in the Dubois form).
    * AD-safe P_cr^(3/4).
    * NaN at start_time = 0.
    * outermost-shock selection and an escape fraction.
    * the finite-difference solver now refuses CRs instead of ignoring them.
  * The standard tests (Pfrommer+17 shock tube with/without injection; two-fluid Sedov against an exact self-similar ODE solution) are ported to `pytests/cosmic_rays/`: 28 pass.
  * Figure: `/export/data/lstorcks/casa_orlando150/work/stage1/cr/casa_cr_1d/casa_cr_1d_fiducial.png`.
* **Library reverse mode:**
  * Changes:
    * the passive-scalar sub-steps use a masked static loop under BACKWARDS;
    * `jnp.clip`-type bounds keep derivative 1;
    * `ad_remat ∈ {none, stage, axis}`;
    * optional straight-through smooth shock latch;
    * `OPTIMAL_BACKEND` is chosen from `jax.devices()`.
  * Checks:
    * forward results are bitwise unchanged;
    * VJP = JVP to 1e-15;
    * the dot-product test passes to 1e-11;
    * on a 64³ x64 A100 run, peak memory is 1.96 GB (62× the state) with axis remat.
  * **Tangent accuracy:** the JVP-vs-FD bias comes entirely from the two deliberate approximations (`weno_ad_frozen_weights`, `ad_tangent_llf_cold_factor`). With both off, the JVP matches FD exactly (32³, 2 yr).
* **Tangent growth** (64³, x64, from the fit-H 2000 state; `stage1/tlm/growth/SUMMARY.md`):
  * **Exact tangents** (frozen weights off, cold-LLF off) match converged FD to 0.3–0.6 % over 2 yr and still match over 5 yr. They explode over 11 yr (×8e3) and 22 yr (×1e13): these are grid-scale growing modes of the cold dense knots.
  * **The approximate tangents** stay bounded over 22 yr (growth 0.74). They track the large-step FD derivative, i.e. a smoothed gradient, and are 20–25 % biased on short windows.
  * **Beyond ~5 yr the true cost is not smooth at small amplitude.**
  * ⇒ 4D-Var design: surrogate-gradient descent with approximate tangents on long windows, then exact-tangent refinement on ≤ 5-yr sub-windows (weak constraint / multiple shooting).
* **Fit fixes** (`casa_xfit`/`casa_pluto_diff`; flags in `FIX_OPTIONS`; `--legacy` reproduces Q2 exactly):
  * The new r_RS estimator matches the tracer to 0.003–0.005 r_FS. Q2 re-measured gives **92″ (r_RS/r_FS 0.58) in 2000**, against 96 ± 10″ observed.
  * Q2 under the corrected likelihood has χ² 2244. One LM step reaches 1773, with the explosion date moving +5.5 yr and the centre moving toward the CoE.
* **Observation model** (v2 tables, not yet wired into the fit):
  * At fixed Q2 parameters, the rebinning fix plus the T_e-history NEI roughly halve the Si and S line excess (Si relative to its continuum 1.33 → 1.17, S 1.21 → 1.09).
  * Fe-K rises to 1.29, a real tension with the cold-electron solution. Fe XXV falls ×0.45.
* **Per-ObsID responses:** CIAO 4.18 with CALDB 4.12.4 (contamination N0016), 22 ObsIDs, in `/export/data/lstorcks/chandra_casa/responses/`.
  * The 2009–2021 kinks and the 2022 soft excess (the acisi_cy22 table is 1/0.72 too soft-efficient for Cas A) are response artefacts.
  * The r < 200″ vignetting offset is 0.87–0.96.
  * The "hard band fades too slowly" residual is mostly *not* a response artefact: the required intrinsic 2000→2019 fading only moves from −20.5 % to −18.6 %.

    * the 4D-Var wins clearly at 2022, 53 vs 110–114, four years out;

## 5d. Stage 4 (2026-09-26): model round, adjoint fix, forward-shock warp

Reports: `/export/data/lstorcks/casa_orlando150/work/stage4/{lib,xfit,warp,review}_RESULT.md`; production and scoring in `stage4/run/` (`tables.md`, `PROGRESS.md`).

* **Library adjoint.** The NaN state gradients came from the passive-scalar WENO backward: `alpha0/alpha_sum` was differentiated through `alpha_sum**-2`, which overflows float32 where the advected `entropy_initial` label reaches −6.7e4 in a collapsed cell.
  * New `_weno_omega_weights_ad` is a custom-JVP wrapper whose exact derivative is written in overflow-safe log form. The forward is bitwise unchanged (sha256 at 32³ and 128³).
  * The NaNs are gone at the reported point (0/7 jitters, was 2/7). A regression test is in `pytests/differentiability/test_ad_tangent_safety.py`.
  * **Spike gradients are the true derivative of rounding-selected hot spots**, which form at a near-vacuum cell next to the reverse shock. No tangent-only fix removes them (five variants tried). They need a forward-robustness decision:
    * bound `entropy_initial`;
    * treat vacuum-edge cells.

    The spike guard remains the mitigation.
* **`casa_xfit` model round** (new defaults; `--stage3` restores the R/R′ likelihood, `--legacy` is unchanged). Δχ² at R′, fixed hydro:

  | change | Δχ² |
  |---|---|
  | measured particle background with the modelled readout streak (`casa_xfit_bkg.py`; the streak is 15–19 % of the annulus) | −26.7 |
  | outline cones 260/270/10 masked (inner-arc detector lock) | −42.9 |
  | cone 300 removed from the PM term | −21.0 |

## 6. Where this leaves the goal, and what to do next

The goal is a state plus parameters at 2000 that reproduces Chandra 2000–2022.

**What exists now:** a validated pipeline.
* **Refit** (casa_xfit v2 + CIAO responses) → **strong-constraint 4D-Var at 2000** (casa_4dvar) → **clean out-of-sample test on 2019/2022**.
* The reconstruction reproduces:
  * the brightness evolution out of sample, far better than its background and than data-only baselines at 4 yr;
  * the spectra, Doppler pattern, reverse and forward shock radii, and CSM density within literature errors.

**What limits it, in order:**
1. **The W15 initial condition's dynamics.** Every refit presses on the knot-convergence bound and wants a denser wind.
   * The state-level 4D-Var relaxes this only over the 2000–2018 window.
   * A calibrated-energy initial condition is the fix (E ≈ 2.4–3e51 at 1681 / 3.4 kpc, CALIBRATION Result 27): a new Orlando run, or rescaling this one.
2. **Discrete solver-path roughness near the reverse shock.** It ends every late 4D-Var stage and produces the gradient spikes; these are hot spots at vacuum-edge cells.
   * It needs a forward-robustness decision: bound `entropy_initial`, and treat the vacuum edge.
3. **Knot-level morphology.** It is not in the 128³ model, and the image-static term is deliberately loose (σ 0.5).
   * 256³ plus a multi-scale likelihood would be next. This needs sharding for the 4D-Var: about 8× the memory.
4. **Held-out outline scoring.** The detector fails on some cones (inner arcs, cones without training radii).
   * Score data and model with one hard-band detector.
5. **The weak-constraint 4D-Var needs a forcing formulation.** The current reduced-rank Q decouples.

**Not needed:** cosmic-ray back-reaction (Stage 1). It is bounded by the γ-ray budget and by the in-house 1D test.

### Is Orlando's state under-energetic?

**Yes.** Independently re-derived by the reviewer.

**Energy budget.**
- The delivered state carries 1.514e51 erg: kinetic 1.216, thermal 0.296, magnetic 0.002.
- Nothing is missing. Only 1.3e47 erg lies outside the PLUTO box, and at most a few per cent was radiated before 146 yr.
- The remap drops 0.3 % of the kinetic energy at 512³ and 1.6 % at 128³.
- This equals W15's quoted 1.5 B. Orlando+21 themselves note the radii come out small because E_exp = 1.5 B is "smaller than … ≈2 B", and Orlando+22 compared the model with Cas A at ≈ 350–370 yr.

**Exact similarity family.**
- The adiabatic ejecta + CSM problem, including the shell, has an exact three-parameter scaling: lengths ×L, times ×T, masses ×M.
- Under it the IC age label becomes 145.5·T.
- The old energy knob `ln_sv` is L = 1, T = 1/s without the relabelling. That is why every fit sat on the explosion-date bound: R2's apparent 1640 is really a ballistic date of 1670.6.

**What the data require.**
- **Key constraint:** matching r_FS(2000), the expansion rate and the admissible age and distance requires E/M_ej = 0.60–0.74 B/M☉. W15 has 0.46.
- **Total energy:** about 2.0–2.3e51 erg for ≈ 3 M☉. It rises to 2.3–2.6e51 once Lee+14's denser wind is added, the density every refit wanted (ln_fw ≈ +0.85).

  | target / assumption | energy (1e51 erg) |
  |---|---|
  | Vink+22 expansion rate | 2.07 |
  | in-house rate | 1.97 |
  | explosion 1671.3 | 1.99 |
  | explosion 1700 | 2.26 |
  | 3.6 kpc | 2.42 |
  | 3.0 kpc | 1.68 |

- **An independent check** from Orlando+22's ejecta velocities (4249 km/s model vs 4820 km/s observed) gives ×1.29 in E/M.
- **Attempts to keep 1.5e51 on GPU:** a thinner wind, no shell, or both. All stay ≥ 5″ short, break the expansion rate and Lee+14, and fail the X-ray brightness. The only other escape is D = 2.83 kpc, 5σ from Alarie+14.

**Implemented.**

- `casa_pluto_diff.transform_fields(sim=…)` is the traced map, with `ic_age` for the relabelled age.

### Are the missing filaments a resolution effect?

**Mostly yes for the hydro model. But the current 4D-Var cannot fit filaments at any resolution.**

**The forward model.** Same Orlando state at 128/256/512³ against Chandra 2000:

| quantity | 128³ | 256³ | 512³ | data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5–15″ contrast variance | 0.015 | 0.036 | 0.054 | 0.126 |
| filament FWHM | 6.7–9.4″ | 4.7″ | 4.7″ | 2.35″ |

- The effective resolution is ≈ 8–11 cells at every resolution. Extrapolating, matching the data needs about 1250–2000³ in the 7 pc box.
- 15–40″ structure is flat with resolution, so that part is large-scale shape.
- The remap keeps 23 % of the delivered state's 0.01–0.03 R_FS structure at 512³, and 0 % at 256³ and 128³.

**What the fit actually sees.** The production 4D-Var images are 26× below the data at 5–15″. In log terms:
- ≈ 60 % of that shortfall is resolution;
- ≈ 40 % is the fit trajectory and the v2 observation chain. The v2 chain removes 2.4× more 5–15″ contrast than v1; this is being investigated.

**What else limits the fit.**
- The 4D-Var control smoothing (14″ sd) passes 3 × 10⁻⁸ of the amplitude at 15″.
- The 31.5″ likelihood blocks with σ_static 0.5 hide half the variance.
- Tangents grow tenfold every ≲ 0.15 yr at 128³ (exact mode), so knot shapes below ~10 cells cannot be fitted pixel by pixel over 22 yr.

**What a 512³ 4D-Var needs:**
- the IC regridded directly at 512³, in a tighter box of ~5.6 pc;
- a finer control: half-resolution grid with ell ≤ 0.5;
- a multi-scale likelihood: add 8–16″ blocks or pixel-level and per-sector structure statistics;
- damped tangents and short windows for the fine scales.
